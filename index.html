RICH

Recovering Interactions and Contacts for Humanoids via Whole-Body Dexterous HOI Retargeting from Imperfect Priors

RICH
OverviewPipelineCoverageTransferCompareRealMethodCitation

Paper

Abstract

High-quality robot data for whole-body dexterous human–object interaction (HOI) remain scarce. Such data must couple body motion with dexterous-hand configurations and hand–object contacts. Retargeting human recordings scales data generation, but embodiment differences amplify contact drift, pose noise, and interpenetration into self-collision, constraint conflicts, and infeasible motion. Whole-body mapping and dexterous contact recovery must therefore be solved jointly to recover executable interactions rather than copy human poses. We introduce RICH, which jointly repairs and retargets imperfect human HOI priors. Its compliant interaction network preserves local interaction structure while relaxing unreliable absolute targets. Task-aware object sampling concentrates geometry near likely contacts, and contact-aware hand repair reconstructs embodiment-compatible palm poses and finger closure. A recoverable per-frame QP combines these objectives with non-penetration, joint-limit, foot-stability, and temporal-continuity constraints to produce references for physics-based control. Across all object categories in OMOMO, RICH reduces the frame rates of foot–ground penetration, hand–object penetration, and self-collision by 93.8–99.6% relative to GMR while retaining a 96.8% solve rate. OmniRetarget yields lower penetration, whereas RICH shortens runtime by 18.9%, raises the solve rate by 9.3 percentage points, and reduces palm–surface distance and the self-collision frame rate by 18.4% and 88.1%, respectively. These results demonstrate a more balanced trade-off among solver efficiency, coverage, contact preservation, and physical validity. Robustness and ablation results further validate the interaction recovery mechanism. Under a shared APPO protocol, policies trained on RICH references achieve the highest scaled mean reward in both single- and bimanual tasks and transfer zero-shot to a physical Unitree G1. Code and data will be released.

Architecture

Method

RICH converts imperfect human HOI priors into robot-ready whole-body interaction references. Instead of copying human poses point by point, it treats the demonstration as noisy interaction intent and jointly recovers feasible body motion, object contact, and dexterous-hand targets.

Compliant interaction network

Preserves local robot-object structure while allowing unreliable absolute targets to yield under constraint conflicts.

Task-aware object surface

Allocates object samples around likely contact regions for collision checking, palm docking, and hand repair.

Contact-aware hand repair

Reconstructs palm docking, palm orientation, and finger closure for the target dexterous hand.

Recoverable per-frame QP

Solves interaction, hand, joint-limit, non-penetration, foot-contact, and temporal-smoothness objectives together.
